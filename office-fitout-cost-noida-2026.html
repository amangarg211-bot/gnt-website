What does an office fit-out cost per sq ft in Noida and NCR in 2026?

As of 2026, G&T Solutions prices office fit-outs in Noida and NCR from ₹1,200 to ₹4,500 per sq ft, across four tiers: basic (₹1,200–₹1,500), mid-range (₹1,500–₹2,000), high-end (₹2,000–₹2,600) and luxury (₹2,600–₹4,500). The right tier depends on the finish level, materials and services the space needs.

What's the difference between basic, mid-range, high-end and luxury office fit-outs?

Basic (₹1,200–₹1,500/sq ft) covers a practical fit-out with standard specifications. Mid-range (₹1,500–₹2,000/sq ft) adds enhanced finishes, better detailing and coordinated workplace requirements. High-end (₹2,000–₹2,600/sq ft) uses premium materials, higher design detailing and upgraded services and furniture. Luxury (₹2,600–₹4,500/sq ft) is a high-spec corporate interior with premium finishes and customised detailing throughout.

What affects the total cost beyond the per-sq-ft rate?

The per-sq-ft rate is a starting point — the final number depends on the specific scope: how much civil and MEP (electrical, plumbing, fire, HVAC) work the space needs, the amount of custom versus standard furniture, the complexity of ceilings and partitions, and any specialised systems like AV or access control. G&T Solutions confirms an exact figure after reviewing the space and the brief.

Does the per-sq-ft cost include furniture?

It depends on the tier and the brief. G&T Solutions scopes furniture — standard or custom — as part of the same single contract as the rest of the fit-out, so it's accounted for in the quote rather than billed separately by a different vendor.
