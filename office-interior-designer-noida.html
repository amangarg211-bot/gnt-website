What's the difference between a turnkey fit-out and hiring separate contractors?

With a turnkey fit-out, one company is contractually responsible for the entire scope — drawings, civil, electrical, furniture and finishing — under a single point of contact. Hiring separate contractors means a client coordinates each trade themselves, which shifts scheduling and quality risk onto them. G&T Solutions works turnkey, taking projects from shop drawings through to the final finishing detail.
