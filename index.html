About G&T

A one-window solution,
from drawing to detail.

G&T Solutions specialises in bringing inanimate objects to life. With a team of experts combining 20 years of experience fabricating commercial fit-outs, workspaces and furniture, we've delivered commercial interiors, workspaces, retail and hospitality projects across the country for clients who care about the details. We take on shop drawings, interior construction, lighting design, custom furniture, signage, artwork and installation — all under one roof — so the ethos of your brand comes alive in the space it lives in.

What We Do

Every layer of the space,
handled in-house.

End-to-end fit-out, from drawings to final finish — design & sourcing, civil & building services, interiors & finishing, all run by one team.
